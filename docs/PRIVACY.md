# Privacy Policy — ReelPilot

Last updated: 2026-10-04

ReelPilot is 100% on-device.

- **Data collected:** none. No accounts, no analytics, no ads SDK.
- **Data stored:** timer interval, toggles, target apps, lifetime counters — in Android DataStore on your phone only.
- **Accessibility data:** foreground package + scroll events are processed in memory to time swipes. Never saved, never transmitted.
- **Network:** the app makes zero network requests.
- **Contact:** replace with your support email before publishing.
